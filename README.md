# Uber-Ride-Analytics-Dashboard-Driving-Demand-Revenue-and-Operational-Insights
A comprehensive business intelligence project leveraging Power Query for ETL processes and Advanced Excel to clean and visualise 10,000+ Uber ride records.

## Project Overview

This project analyses the ride hailing data to uncover key insight into customer demand, operational efficiency, driver performance and revenue generation.

Using Microsoft Excel (Pivot Tables and Dashboarding),power query, I developed an interactive business intelligence dashboard that transforms  an online  raw ride request into actionable insights for decision making.
The dashboard was designed to support a strategic decisions in:

Driver allocation
Demand forecasting
Revenue Optimisation
Customer experience improvement

## Business Problem

Ride hailing platform must balance customer demand and  with customer supply while maintaining high quality service and maximising revenue.

This project aims to answer the key business questions:

When is ride demand the highest?
Where are the most profitable location?
How efficient are ride completion?
What factor impact cancellation and customer satisfaction? 

## Dataset Description

The dataset consists of ride request records including:

Request details (date, time, hour)
Pickup and drop off locations
Ride type (UberX, UberXL, Comfort)
Driver and customer IDs
Ride status (Completed, Cancelled, No Driver)
Revenue and distance metrics
Customer ratings and feedback
Cancellation reasons

## Key Business Insights & Strategic Recommendations

Core Findings: The Success Gap
The analysis of 10,000 ride requests revealed a critical performance gap:

Completion Rate: Only 67% of ride requests are successfully completed.  

Failure Rate: 33% of requests failed due to cancellations or driver unavailability.  

Implication: There is a significant structural mismatch between real time demand and driver supply, particularly during peak windows.  

## Supporting Analytical Findings
Peak Demand Cycles: Ride requests reach their maximum volume during evening and night periods.  

Cancellation Correlation: A large portion of failed rides is directly correlated with high-demand hours, suggesting system strain.  

Operational Inefficiency: "No Driver Available" cases serve as a primary KPI for identifying supply-side gaps.  

Revenue Anomalies: Data modeling shows that high demand locations do not always equate to highest revenue, indicating a need for better route-value optimization.  

Customer Sentiment: Analysis of feedback shows that low ratings are strictly associated with failed or delayed service.  

## Business Impact
Revenue Leakage: Significant lost revenue from unfulfilled ride requests.  

Brand Erosion: Reduced customer satisfaction leads to lower lifetime value (LTV).  

Resource Inefficiency: Suboptimal driver utilization during off peak hours.  


## Strategic Recommendations
To enhance business performance and efficiency, I recommend the following data-driven actions:
Supply Side Optimization: Implement a dynamic shift schedule to increase driver availability during evening and night peak periods.  

Incentive Modeling: Introduce targeted driver incentive programs specifically for high-demand, high-cancellation hotspots to stabilize the completion rate.  

Dynamic Pricing: Utilize the identified demand trends to implement dynamic pricing strategies, balancing supply and demand while maximizing revenue per trip.  

Strategic Growth: Realign marketing and operational focus toward high-performing locations and premium ride types (e.g. Comfort, UberXL) to drive sustainable revenue growth.
